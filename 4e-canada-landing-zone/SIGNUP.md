# Signing up for the Azure free account

Follow these rules in order. Each has one sentence on why, so you can explain it in an interview.

1. **The Azure free account is for new customers only.** Microsoft gives the credit to people who have never had an Azure free account, so an e-mail, card or phone that was used before can be refused.
2. **Expect a credit card and a phone number check.** Sign-up checks that you are a real person, and it asks for a credit card and a phone number. `TODO(verify)`: whether the card is only checked or also charged, as stated on the sign-up page that day.
3. **You get about $200 of credit that lasts 30 days.** Plan the work inside that window, because the credit is meant to be used up within it. `TODO(verify)`: the current amount, the length of the period and the sign-up URL on the day you sign up, because Microsoft changes these terms.
4. **Never upgrade to pay-as-you-go during the sprint.** Pay-as-you-go bills your card for whatever you leave running, so staying on the free trial keeps a mistake inside the credit.
5. **Sign up with the Track 3 tenant's admin account.** A *tenant* is your organisation's private space in Microsoft's cloud; using the Track 3 tenant's admin account means one tenant holds everything (the identity work in Track 3 and the cloud work here).
6. **Start the account on the day you configure 4E, not earlier.** The 30 days start counting at sign-up, so signing up weeks before you apply `README.md` wastes your credit.

## If sign-up is refused
This can happen if, for example, the card or the phone number was already used for a free account. Stop and decide with Claude. The two options are:

- **Azure for Students**, if you have a school e-mail address. It needs no credit card. `TODO(verify)`: current eligibility and terms.
- **Pay-as-you-go**, guarded by the $10 budget alert from `BUDGET-AND-ALERTS.md`. 4E and 4H cost cents, so the alert is the safety net.

Whichever you choose, record the choice in `docs/CAVEATS.md` (one line, in the format that file explains).
